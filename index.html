How It Works

Install the extension. Refresh your AI tabs. A small red bell appears on each page.

Send a prompt and switch away. AgentPing watches for the AI to finish and pings you with a sound and desktop notification.

Features

5 Sounds

Gentle Chime, Three-Note, Warm Bell, Soft Hum, Sharp Ping. Pick your favorite.

Phone Alerts

Get push notifications on your phone via ntfy.sh. Free, no account needed.

Multi-Tab

Monitors all your AI tabs at once. See which are running or done in the popup.

Zero Data

No analytics. No tracking. No servers. Everything stays on your device.

Phone Alerts Setup

For notifications when your screen is off:

  • Install the ntfy app on your phone
  • Subscribe to a topic name (e.g., my-agentping-alerts)
  • Enter the same topic in AgentPing's popup
  • Test with the "Test Phone Alert" button
