Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

What AgentPing Does

AgentPing monitors AI chat tabs (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok) to detect when they finish generating a response, then alerts you with a sound and notification.

Data We Collect

None. AgentPing does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not read your AI conversations or prompts
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not send any data to our servers
  • We have no analytics, telemetry, or tracking

Data Stored Locally

AgentPing stores the following data only on your device using Chrome's built-in storage:

  • Settings: Your volume, sound choice, and platform toggles
  • Tab states: Which AI tabs are running/completed (cleared when browser closes)
  • Bell position: Where you dragged the bell button on each site

This data never leaves your device.

Phone Alerts (Optional)

If you enable phone alerts, AgentPing sends a short notification message to ntfy.sh (a free, open-source push service). The message contains only the AI platform name and tab title — no conversation content. This is entirely optional and off by default.

Permissions Explained

  • tabs: To detect which AI tabs are open and show them in the popup
  • notifications: To show desktop alerts when AI finishes
  • storage: To save your settings locally
  • activeTab: To interact with the current tab
  • offscreen: To play notification sounds in the background

Third-Party Services

AgentPing connects to Google Fonts to load the Fraunces and Outfit typefaces in the popup. No other external services are used unless you opt into phone alerts (ntfy.sh).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Since we collect no data, changes would only reflect new features.
